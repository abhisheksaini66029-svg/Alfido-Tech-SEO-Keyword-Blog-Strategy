# Alfido-Tech-SEO-Keyword-Blog-Strategy
 **SEO Keyword & Blog Strategy created as Task 2 of my Alfido Tech internship. This project focuses on improving website visibility and organic reach through SEO. It includes 15–20 high-value keywords related to internships and IT services, covering areas such as software development, web development, AI, data analytics, cloud computing, and IT consulting. The project also includes 3 SEO-focused blog topic suggestions and a 300–400 word sample blog article designed to attract students, freshers, and potential clients. This task helped me understand keyword research, search intent, content planning, on-page SEO, audience targeting, and SEO-driven content strategy.                                                    
 
3 Blog Topics Suggestions :-

Topic 1: AICTE-Approved Internships Explained — Why Verification Matters for Students
•  Target keyword: AICTE approved internship
•  Intent: Informational → Transactional (educates, then funnels to application page)
•  Angle: Alfido Tech is officially listed on the AICTE National Internship Portal — a genuine trust signal most competitors can't claim. This post explains what that listing means and how students can verify it themselves.


Topic 2: Python vs. Web Development Internship — Which Should You Choose in 2026?
•  Target keywords: python developer internship, web development internship online
•  Intent: Informational (comparison content that ranks well and nurtures undecided applicants)
•  Angle: A practical, skills-first comparison of the two most-searched internship domains, closing with a CTA to explore all 19 domains on the Domains page.


Topic 3: 5 Reasons Small Businesses Need Professional SEO Services in 2026
•  Target keyword: SEO optimization services India
•  Intent: Transactional (targets the IT-services side of the business, not just internship seekers)
•  Angle: Positions Alfido Tech's SEO service for small business owners and startups, using practical, non-jargon reasons to invest in SEO now.
